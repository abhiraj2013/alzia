ABOUT CIVA

CIVA is being developed as a public-facing digital service under Robaar, an Alzia department.

Its purpose is to provide a simple place for people to share public problems, view issues raised by others and support peaceful civic participation.

CIVA focuses on real-world areas such as roads, water, electricity, education, environment, transport and other public facilities.
